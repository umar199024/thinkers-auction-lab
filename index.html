Auction simulator

Every block clears at one price for everyone.

In a CCA, supply is released block by block. Every bid states a budget and a max price, and is spread over the blocks left. Each block clears at ONE price for everyone: the price where demand meets that block's supply. If the price rises above your max, your bid stops filling.
